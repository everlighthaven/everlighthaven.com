OUR MISSION

Safety, care, and opportunity.

Everlight Children & Family Haven of Texas is being developed to support vulnerable children and families through safe care, education support, health resources, emotional support, and opportunities to grow.

A future where children feel safe, valued, and supported, and families have access to resources and opportunities that strengthen their futures.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Where the project stands today.

Is Everlight Haven currently operating?

No. This website describes a developing project and does not represent an already-operating children's home.

Are children currently being accepted or placed?

No. The project does not make private placement promises. Any future placement process would need to follow applicable Texas child-welfare, licensing, safeguarding, and legal requirements.

Can the public support the project?

Yes. People interested in helping the project move toward development can contact the project team using the contribution button above. Any funds should be handled through an appropriate, verified account under responsible adult leadership, with clear records and accurate information about the project's current legal and tax status.
