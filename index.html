Our Mission

Everlight Children & Family Haven of Texas is being developed to support vulnerable children and families through safe care, education support, health resources, emotional support, and opportunities to grow.

What We Hope to Provide

Safe Accommodation

A secure, caring environment for children placed through appropriate child-welfare and licensed systems.

Nutritious Meals

Reliable access to nourishing food and healthy routines.

Education Support

Help with learning, school participation, and future opportunities.

Healthcare

Support for appropriate medical, dental, and wellbeing needs.

Emotional Support

Compassionate support and connections to qualified professionals when needed.

Growth & Recreation

Activities that encourage confidence, creativity, friendship, and healthy development.
